Hello and welcome,

Thank you for joining the FOSS4G 2027 mailing list. FOSS4G, Free and Open Source Software for Geospatial, is the global conference of the open source geospatial community, hosted in partnership with OSGeo. In August 2027 it comes to Bristol, United Kingdom, hosted by the University of Bristol.

What to expect

We will send you our newsletter whenever there is news to share, including:

  • Registration opening dates and ticket prices
  • The conference programme, workshops and social events
  • The Travel Grant Programme
  • Sponsorship and volunteering opportunities

Sponsorship helps us keep tickets affordable for students and community members and funds our Travel Grant Programme. Smaller organisations and individuals can also support the conference through Adopt-a-ticket.
