Getting ready for Bristol
August 23–29, 2027 · Bristol, United Kingdom

Hello FOSS4G community,

Welcome to the first FOSS4G 2027 newsletter! Planning for next summer is well under way. In this edition we ask parents to pre-register for childcare, invite sponsors to support the conference, share an update on ticket pricing and registration, and give you a first taste of our host city.

Bringing your children? Pre-register for childcare

We will provide childcare for dependents of delegates at FOSS4G 2027. To organise the right level of support we need a good idea of numbers early on.

If you are planning to attend and are considering bringing your children, please fill out our short pre-registration form. It does not commit you to anything, but it helps us plan and budget for the service.

Tell us about your childcare needs

Let us know how many children you plan to bring, their ages and which days you need.

Pre-register for childcare

Sponsor the childcare service. We are looking for a sponsor for the childcare service, which we expect to cost around £5,000 depending on the numbers. It is a great way to help make FOSS4G accessible to parents and carers in our community. If your organisation is interested, please get in touch at sponsor@foss4g.org.

Call for sponsors

We have had great feedback from previous FOSS4G sponsors and are delighted that a few sponsors have already confirmed their support. Thank you!

FOSS4G brings together open source geospatial developers, users, decision makers and researchers from around the world. Sponsorship helps us keep tickets affordable for students and community members and funds our travel grant programme. Packages range from Bronze to Diamond. Find all the details in our prospectus.

Super early bird registration opens in January

We will review our conference budget before the holidays to set ticket prices for the super early bird, early bird and regular tickets. Super early bird registration is expected to open in January 2027. We will announce prices and the opening date here first, so stay subscribed.

Welcome to Bristol

If ever there was a city with a story to tell, it’s Bristol. Set among the hills of South West England, Bristol has an independent spirit shaped by its passionate locals.

It is a city of engineers and makers: home to Brunel’s Clifton Suspension Bridge and the SS Great Britain, the place where Concorde was built, and a hub for aerospace, technology and creative industries. Its harbourside, colourful terraces and world-famous street art make it a wonderful place to explore between sessions.

The conference will be hosted by the University of Bristol, with workshops on 23–24 August, the main conference from 25 to 27 August in the Wills Memorial and Chemistry buildings, and community events and a code sprint over the weekend. Our gala dinner will take place at Ashton Gate Stadium.
